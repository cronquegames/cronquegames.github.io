Questions

How do I play?
Hold anywhere to glow and sink. Let go to go dark and hide. Bank your light at a shrine to keep it, or dive on for more.

Do I have to start from 0 m every time?
No. Each zone you reach adds a depth button above Dive. Swipe that row and tap where you want to start.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your iPhone. To move it to another iPhone, open Settings › Transfer save, copy the code, and paste it on the other phone.

How do I get the Companion?
Find both Legendaries in the Chest (the Galaxy trail and the Supernova shrine effect). The Companion unlocks for free and gives you a free revive in every dive.

How does the Chest work?
Keys are hidden in the sea, 30 of them between 0 and 40,000 m. Each key opens the Chest once and gives one trail or shrine effect you don't have yet. Each key can only be found once; the Chest shows how deep the next one is.

What's the trophy button in the menu?
Your records: deepest dive, bosses escaped, creatures met and more. It opens once you reach 31,000 m.

Does Lull need the internet?
No, it plays completely offline.

Found a bug?
Use Settings › Report a bug in the game, or email us.
