Privacy Policy

Lull · last updated October 2026

Lull is a single-player game made by Dmitrii Manevich ("we").

The short version

Lull doesn't collect any personal data. There's no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no in-app purchases. The game doesn't send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

Where your progress is kept

  • Your game progress and settings are saved on your iPhone.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy of your game progress is also kept in your own iCloud account, so a new iPhone or a reinstall picks up where you left off. It's stored by Apple in your account; we can't see or access it. You can turn this off in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
  • If you use Game Center, your best depth, Boss Rush time and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, and shown on its leaderboards. This is Apple's service, covered by Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive any personal data from it.
  • The diver name you choose is stored only on your iPhone. It isn't sent anywhere or shown to other players.
  • The "Transfer save" code is made on your iPhone and goes only where you paste it. We never receive it.

If you email us

If you write to us, or use Report a bug (which opens your own email app with a message you can read before sending), we receive what you send, including your email address. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, and we delete it when it's no longer needed.

Children

Lull doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
